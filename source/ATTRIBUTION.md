The structural surah, juz, page, and sajdah metadata is derived from Tanzil Quran Metadata version 1.0. The supplied `big-quran.pdf` was used as an edition and visual reference for **مصحف المدينة النبوية**; it is not bundled into the application.

The complete original notice is preserved in `source/TANZIL_LICENSE.txt` and its attribution is shown in the application’s About screen.
