# Waqf Marks Content Notes

The in-app lesson presents the six common Hafs-style Mushaf markers requested by the user: **م**, **لا**, **ج**, **قلى**, **صلى**, and **ۛ ۛ**. The Arabic symbols and Qur'anic example text remain Arabic in every app language; the concise explanatory interface remains localized.

The full educational Arabic material supplied in the six screenshots is included once, with only screenshot-overlap repetitions removed. It comprises the introductory paragraph on Mushaf stop marks, the definition and ruling of waqf, the four types of waqf, the stated importance points, and the detailed explanation and Qur'anic example for each of the six marks. Qur'anic examples are drawn from the app's bundled Uthmani text rather than typed or translated separately.

| Symbol | Arabic label retained in the lesson | Concise explanation |
|---|---|---|
| م | وقف لازم | Stop to avoid a misleading connection in meaning. |
| لا | لا تقف | Continue; stopping here can interrupt the intended grammatical meaning. |
| ج | وقف جائز | Both stopping and continuing are permitted. |
| قلى | الوقف أولى | Stopping is preferred, while continuing remains permitted. |
| صلى | الوصل أولى | Continuing is preferred, while stopping remains permitted. |
| ۛ ۛ | وقف المعانقة | Stop at one of the paired positions, not both. |

The screen will include a brief educational notice that notation may vary by Mushaf edition and that a qualified teacher remains the best source for recitation correction.
